# SOMA Website Content Specification

## Credential Priority

Surface these credentials consistently on the Homepage, Corporate page, and About page before championship or emotional biography content:

- Harvard Medical School stress-management training
- Master's in Personnel Management
- Ayush Level 3 certification
- 22 years embedded at Tata Consultancy Services, Pune

## About Page Order

The About page should lead with HR decision-maker proof, then corporate credibility, then championship and biography:

1. Standing photo of Soma with key professional credentials above the fold.
2. Harvard, Personnel Management, Ayush Level 3, and TCS proof block.
3. The exact TCS six-month story quote:
   "What started as a brief six-month wellness intervention at Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) in 2001 evolved into a monumental 22-year tenure. She embedded stillness into the architecture of Pune’s IT boom, supporting the workforce through an era of endless acceleration."
4. Tata Steel connection, including J. J. Irani, Russi Modi, and magazine feature proof with supporting photos.
5. Championship background: All-India titles, state honours, and Biswanath Ghosh lineage.
6. Biographical story: Jamshedpur, father’s athletic background, and early morning training narrative.

## Testimonials

Avoid a large generic testimonial carousel on About. Use 3-5 curated, named, role-identified, outcome-focused testimonials. Include at minimum:

- One TCS associate
- One senior HR or senior organisational decision-maker
- One individual client

## Imagery

Use current and enhanced images confidently without in-page AI imagery disclaimers. Do not add AI-image disclosure copy to public pages.
