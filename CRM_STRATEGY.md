# Soma Wellness CRM & Lead Strategy

This document outlines the strategic logic for scoring, segmenting, and managing leads captured on the Soma Wellness platform.

---

## 1. Lead Scoring Model (0–100)

We calculate a priority score for every incoming inquiry to help Soma triage her outreach.

### Component 1: Bio-Signature Score (Max 50 pts)
*Reflects physiological urgency.*
- **30–40 (Burnout Zone)**: 45–50 pts
- **20–29 (Threshold Zone)**: 28–44 pts
- **10–19 (Awareness Zone)**: 10–27 pts
- **0–9 (Low urgency)**: 0–9 pts

### Component 2: Professional Context (Max 25 pts)
*Reflects potential contract size and role impact.*
- **HR Head / CHO / L&D**: 25 pts
- **CEO / Founder / C-suite**: 22 pts
- **Senior Manager / Director**: 16 pts
- **Individual Professional**: 10 pts

### Component 3: Intake Depth (Max 15 pts)
*Reflects buying intent and commitment.*
- **Full Completion** (Emotional goals + Sleep data): 15 pts
- **Partial Completion**: 8 pts
- **Assessment Only**: 0 pts

### Component 4: Specific Program Inquiry (Max 10 pts)
*Reflects conversion readiness.*
- **Specific Program Named** (e.g., Sleep Architecture): 10 pts
- **General Inquiry**: 5 pts

---

## 2. Urgency Tiers & Actions

| Total Score | Tier | Required Action |
|---|---|---|
| **75–100** | 🔥 **Burnout Zone** | Personal outreach within 24 hours. |
| **45–74** | ⚠️ **Threshold Zone** | Personalized email within 48 hours. |
| **0–44** | 🌱 **Awareness Zone** | 21-day automated nurture sequence. |

---

## 3. Segmentation Strategy

### Institutional (B2B)
- **Trigger**: Professional Context = HR, CHO, L&D or Corporate Inquiry.
- **Focus**: ROI, Retention, Team Vitality.
- **Goal**: Strategy Design Call / Pilot Program.

### Individual (B2C)
- **Trigger**: Program Inquiries (Sleep, Cognitive, etc.) or General Inquiry.
- **Focus**: Personal recovery, Soma's philosophy, Emotional Resonance.
- **Goal**: 1:1 Consultation / Cohort Enrollment.

---

## 4. Lead Magnets (Sequenced)
1. **Personalized Bio-Signature Report**: Auto-generated based on assessment score.
2. **The 7-Night Sleep Reset**: 7-day audio protocol for sleep-deprived leads.
3. **Organizational Vitality Audit**: High-end B2B whitepaper for HR leads.
